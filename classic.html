Why we're here

Financial confidence should be accessible to everyone.

Learning to manage money can feel overwhelming, especially when you're preparing to live independently. We make the basics easier to understand and practice, without jargon or judgment.

About the project

A small project with a meaningful purpose.

The Next Chapter Project is an independent student-led educational initiative in its early pilot stage. It aims to make practical financial knowledge easier to access, with particular attention to young people navigating transitions toward independence.

We're developing these resources and hope to improve them with feedback from young adults and community organizations. No partnerships or program outcomes are claimed at this stage.

Help shape the project

This is a pilot. Your feedback matters.

We're testing whether these lessons and tools are clear and useful. If you work with young adults or support financial education, we'd welcome feedback through an appropriate organization-led process.

We do not ask visitors to share names, financial details, or foster-care histories. No partner endorsements or measured outcomes are claimed.

How impact will be measured

We plan to document resource reviews, pilot participation, anonymous learning checks, and improvements made in response to feedback. Results will be published only when verified and appropriate to share.
